About Me

Driven by curiosity to build systems that think, reason, and empower.

Sirish Bikram Baniya

AI / ML Student & Researcher

Deep Learning LLMs Autonomous Agents

I'm an AI student at the National Institute of Engineering and Technology, based in Kathmandu, Nepal, currently building my foundation in artificial intelligence, robotics, computer hardware, software systems, and emerging technologies. My academic path — from United Academy, Lalitpur to Sundarban School of Science, and now into AI at NIET — has been paired with intensive hands-on training that goes beyond the classroom: a 52-hour robotics workshop covering IoT, machine learning, and 3D printing; a robotics exhibition at United Robo Fest 3.0; and a diploma-level deep dive into computer hardware and software over six months. What draws me to this field is the challenge of bridging theory and practice — understanding not just how systems work, but how to build them, break them, and make them better. Alongside my technical work, I've stayed engaged with my community through youth development programs and even picked up practical financial literacy through a short share market training program. I'd describe myself as adaptable, analytical, and endlessly curious — someone who leads well in team settings, solves problems methodically, and never stops treating learning as a project in progress.

Currently, my studies and personal research focus on large language models, reasoning architectures, and multi-agent collaboration. I am passionate about bridging the gap between theoretical machine learning breakthroughs and robust, reliable applications that people can trust.

What drives me is the belief that AI should augment human capability and accelerate scientific discovery. When I am not training models or reading latest arXiv papers, I write technical notes, build open-source experiments, and participate in hackathons.
